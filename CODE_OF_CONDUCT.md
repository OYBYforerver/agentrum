# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
Agentrum community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behave professionally and constructively: use welcoming and inclusive
language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive criticism, and
focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and publishing
others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainers via the
contact channels in the repository owner profile. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated. Maintainers who do not follow this Code of
Conduct may be removed from the project team.
